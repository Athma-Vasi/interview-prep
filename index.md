# Behavioral Questions UBC Operating Engineer Interview

## Tell me about yourself.

### What can I do for UBC?

I am a conscientious, safe, and reliable 4th class power engineer with
experience working in various industrial settings, including well-sites,
refineries, and oil batteries. I am excited about the opportunity to contribute
to UBC's sustainability goals and ensure the safe and efficient operation of its
boilers, HVAC, as well as other mechanical equipment.

### What makes me sure I can do the job?

I have a strong background in power engineering, with hands-on experience in
maintaining and operating various mechanical systems. My commitment to safety
and reliability, combined with my ability to adapt to different industrial
environments, makes me confident that I can effectively contribute to UBC's
operations and sustainability initiatives.

### Why should they believe me?

I have a proven track record of successfully working in demanding industrial
settings, where safety and efficiency are paramount. My experience and
dedication to my profession have been recognized by my peers and supervisors,
and I am committed to upholding the high standards expected at UBC.
Additionally, I am eager to learn and grow within the university's environment,
which further demonstrates my commitment to contributing positively to UBC's
goals.

### Why am I interested in working for UBC?

UBC is a prestigious university known for its commitment to sustainability,
innovation, and inclusivity. I am excited about the opportunity to contribute to
UBC's sustainability goals and ensure the safe and efficient operation of its
boilers, HVAC, as well as other mechanical equipment. Working at UBC would allow
me to be part of an institution that values environmental responsibility and
community engagement, which aligns with my personal and professional values.

### Why should they choose me over other candidates?

My combination of practical experience, commitment to safety, and passion for
sustainability sets me apart from other candidates. I have a proven ability to
adapt to various industrial settings and a strong desire to contribute to UBC's
goals. My dedication to continuous learning and improvement ensures that I will
be an asset to the university, helping to maintain and enhance its mechanical
systems while supporting its sustainability initiatives.
